Good to know

Before you get started.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

The Windows app works without an account, including offline writing, searching, and exports. Browser mode requires a Google account; downloaded notes can be edited offline after the app and notes have been cached. Backups resume when you reconnect.

Can I use NoteX on a tablet or phone?

Yes. Open the web app in your browser and sign in with Google. The interface adapts to touch screens; on phones, use portrait orientation. Read the tablet and phone guide →

Can I move or back up my notes?

Yes. On desktop, export a complete workspace with its attached files as a .notex package, or a single note as .notex-note. A full-workspace import replaces the current library. You can also back up an account library to Google Drive. Read the import and backup guide →

Is AI required to use NoteX?

No. Local MCP is optional and starts only when you enable it. It connects compatible clients to your library while NoteX is open. Content read by an AI client is subject to that client's own privacy settings.
