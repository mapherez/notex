# A workspace for knowledge you return to

NoteX is an app for keeping useful information together on Windows and in the browser: project decisions, recipes, references, study material, and anything you want to find again. Notes can contain several titled blocks, rich text, images, and attached files.

Your working library stays on your computer. You can use the desktop app without an account or an internet connection.

## Start with a note

You do not need to design an entire filing system before writing. Create a note, give it a title, and add your content. Collections and tags can come later.

The [first-note guide](quickstart.md) walks through installation, writing, and finding what you saved.

## Give information some structure

- **Blocks** divide a note into sections that you can title and reorder.
- **Collections** group notes under a main subject or project.
- **Tags** connect notes across collections.
- **Favorites and pins** keep frequently used notes close.
- **Search** looks beyond the title, including block content and organization.

Read about [notes and blocks](blocks.md) or [collections and tags](collections-tags.md).

## Keep your data portable

NoteX saves edits locally as you work. You can also export a complete workspace, including attached files, or export one note to share or keep separately.

Local autosave and backups serve different purposes. A backup kept elsewhere helps if your computer or disk fails. See [import, export, and backups](import-export.md) for the two package formats and the import behavior.

## Connect an AI client when you need it

The optional local MCP server lets a compatible AI client search, read, create, edit, and organize notes. You decide when to start it, and NoteX must remain open while it is being used.

Start with [connecting an AI client](mcp-setup.md), then explore [tools and workflows](mcp-workflows.md).

## Back up to Google Drive and use the browser

In NoteX 2.3.0, connect your Google account for backups to your own Drive.
Desktop login is optional; browser mode requires login and stores downloaded
notes locally in your browser. See [Google Drive and browser mode](google-drive-web.md)
for automatic backups, accounts, downloads, and offline work.

Local MCP and full-workspace import/export controls are desktop features.
Hosted MCP and tablet/mobile layouts are planned separately.
