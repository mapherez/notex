# Favorites, pins & trash

Use favorites and pins to reach important notes faster. Use trash when removing a note from the active library without immediately deleting it permanently.

## Favorites

Mark a note with its star to add it to Favorites. This gives you a view of notes you regularly return to, without changing their collection or tags.

Removing the favorite mark does not remove the note from your library.

## Pins

Pin notes that should stay easy to reach in your lists. Pinned notes have their own section and can be manually reordered.

Favorites and pins are independent: a note can use either or both. A useful distinction is to favorite long-term references and pin notes relevant to your current work.

## Recent notes

Recent notes are ordered by the most recent edit, from newest to oldest. Opening a note without changing it does not count as an update.

Use recent notes to return to what you were working on; use favorites and pins when you want an explicit selection of important notes.

## Move a note to trash

Moving a note to trash removes it from the active library. Open Trash if you want to restore it or delete it permanently.

Restoring a note returns it to the active library. Permanent deletion removes the note and its associated local attachment files.

## Clear trash

Clearing trash permanently deletes its contents. Check the list before confirming.

> Permanent deletion cannot be undone through Trash. Keep an exported backup if you need a recoverable copy outside the working library.
